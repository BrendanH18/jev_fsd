Summary

Challenges and road tests

Each challenge has its own street, seed, weather, time and traffic, on the lockstep clock. A pass means arriving with no collision, red light, missed stop or failure to yield, and less than a second off the road. Parking and scenario-specific checks also apply. Path scores use measurements from the car’s driven corridor.

The same, on the open track

The same drives, but the safety filter flags a manoeuvre that breaks a road rule instead of removing it, so the driver's own judgment decides. A predicted collision is still removed and the emergency brake still runs. Rules and the cautious agent keep to the unflagged manoeuvres, so they drive it exactly as the guarded track.

City suite

Seeded routes with every free-drive feature on (suite seed 1, 40 traffic cars, lockstep clock): twelve on Kitsilano in each weather and two on each other map. Each cell is the newest committed run.
