## Open

**23:30 fog `s1-6`, West Broadway (`e247`), 206.2 s.** Not night-specific: by night or day, fog's 28 m is
what the car sees. Turning left at a junction without a sign (the car on the major road), it came at
9.7 m/s and entered the turn at 8–9 m/s; oncoming `car_36` (11.1 m/s) came out of the fog when the car
could no longer stop short of its path, it braked and stopped with its nose 1.3 m into that lane, and
`car_36` ran into it (fault "other"). In 28 m of fog no left turn in front of 11 m/s traffic can be
checked: from the wait point the car needs about 3.5 s, in which that traffic covers 45 m.

A fix was tried and left out: where the car cannot see along an oncoming lane, from the wait point, as
far as traffic covers while it takes the turn at 6 m/s, it comes to the wait point slowly enough to stop
there and turns from there once nothing it can see is coming (in practice fog only; rain and snow see
far enough on a 50 km/h street). It removes this failure, and a closed-loop check of it passes where
the old behaviour collides or cuts off the oncoming car. But it makes the car crawl at every unprotected
left in fog, and the new timings exposed other faults: daytime city fog went from 12/12 to 8/12 (failures
to yield to emergency vehicles while waiting, an all-way stop, a 573 s timeout), and 23:30 fog stayed at
10/12 with different routes failing. Among them: stopping for an ambulance at its destination on Larch
Street, the car's pull-over is rejected as a collision with `parked_834` although `parkedRoom` allowed
0.3 m. These need fixing first; taking the fog left turn up then should be a ROADMAP item of its own.
