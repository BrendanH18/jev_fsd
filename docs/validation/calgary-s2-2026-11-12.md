The bus fix changes Kitsilano's city traffic, which brought out the same pull-over fault there.
Kitsilano city `s1-12` (passing on the untouched tree) failed to yield to `ambulance_2` on West Broadway
at 263.4 s. Called on at 3.7 m/s in the inner lane, with the ambulance oncoming astride the centre line,
the car was told to cross into the curb lane with its stop about 8 m ahead. It reached the stop at −22°,
0.6 m left of the curb lane's middle, which the audit (measuring in the lane the car is now in) does not
count as pulled over. A junction 10 m ahead caps the longer stop, so the car now pulls over 0.3 m in the
inner lane, and the ambulance squeezes past at 4 m/s. In the Calgary queue the stop is capped short of
`sig2` too, so the car would no longer set off across the lanes.

`test_school_buses.mjs` counted, at the end of its seeded four-minute Kitsilano soak, a bus that had stopped
and was still about with its reds off. With the fix, `school_bus_1` (right from `e247` into `e273`) and
`school_bus_2` (left from `e191` into `e327`) each give up a turn whose roll touches a parked car, so the
soak plays out differently, and at its last instant every bus that stopped has either left or has its reds
on. On the untouched tree those turns were made by driving through the parked cars: in the same soak four
school buses overlap a parked car (`school_bus_1` with `parked_1636` on `e273` at 81.7 s, as the roll
predicts). Traffic overlapping parked cars is not audited, so this went unseen. In Calgary the bus's
tangent body check happened to catch it and the bus stood still instead. The check now records any stop
whose reds go off again with the bus still out, over the whole soak.

## Open

- **The pull-over audit and lane crossings.** The audit measures "pulled over" in the lane the car is in.
  A car that has crossed most of the way into the curb lane is further right than one 0.2 m over in its
  own lane, but is not counted. Pulling over now avoids starting crossings it cannot finish; the audit
  was left as it is.
- **Pulling over in the inner lane holds up a vehicle passing astride the centre line.** Short of a
  junction, the car now stays in its lane. On a two-way street the emergency vehicle then squeezes past
  slowly, or, coming from behind, is held up until the car is told to drive on ahead
  (`test_emergency_cross_room.mjs`, no failure either way).
- **Other long vehicles.** Transit buses (fixed runs), snow plows, collection trucks and emergency
  vehicles can sweep over parked cars at corners in the same way. Only school buses, which choose their
  own turns, re-plan here. Traffic overlapping parked cars is not audited anywhere.
