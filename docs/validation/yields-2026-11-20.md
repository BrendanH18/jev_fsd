| Winnipeg city dry `s1-1`: failure to yield to `ambulance_1`, Martha Street (`e200`), 85.3 s | **The audit.** Martha Street (two lanes in the car's direction) gives way at its next junction, which has no sign (`MapData.priorityOf`, rule `minor`). When the ambulance called from behind, the car was creeping at 1.3 m/s, 3 m past its give-way line, towards where it looks from (`sPeek`, 4.6 m past the line). It stopped there in the curb lane, short of the crossing street, and held still while the ambulance went by in the other lane. Its plan was right: the stop it planned 0.3 m ahead left no room to pull over. But the audit excuses a held stop only at a stop sign's or signal's own line (`road.edge.control`, within 2 m short to 1.5 m over), and the car's front was outside the 8 m box round the junction's centre that also excuses it. So the ambulance drawing level counted. | `auditEmergency` (`sim/world.js`): giving way at a junction without a sign or signal (not one its street has priority at), or at a stop sign, a stop held from 2 m short of the line to 1.5 m past the point the car looks from counts as a line stop; away from the line, only within 8 m short of that point (`AUDIT_EV_LOOK_M`), so a stop sign set back from its junction does not excuse a stop mid-block. That holds when the car is already stopped there at the call, or crawls there slowly enough to stop (as `lineApproach`), from the line or from near the look point. *Amended after review, 20 November: the 8 m bound, priority approaches left out, and a crawl just over a line judged at the look point too.* Leaving the stop by more than 2 m, a stop made only after a missed chance to pull over, and holding up the vehicle are judged as before. The drive is unchanged; only the verdict changes. | `test_emergency_give_way_look.mjs` |
| Kitsilano city dry 06:30 `s1-4`: failure to yield to `ambulance_1`, Vine Street (`e375`), 115.3 s | **The car.** The ambulance called just after the car had turned in from West Broadway, 0.42 m left of its lane's centre, at 4.7 m/s. The pull-over was planned with 9 m to steer in, but for 2.3 s its forward simulations came within 0.3 m of `parked_2333`'s corner and rejected it as a collision. The car slowed in its lane and began to pull over only 3.6 m short of its stop. Pursuing its ordinary lookahead, it stopped with its rear axle 0.17 m right of the lane's centre, yawed 2.8° to the kerb. That is short of the 0.19 m the audit asks for beside those cars, and the audit measures the rear axle on purpose (`test_emergency_control_line.mjs`). | `emergencyYield` (`brain/sensors.js`): a pull-over still not begun a second after it was planned (`EV_LATE_S`), with less than half the 8 m steering distance left, finishes its move within its stop (`steer_at_stop`, as a pull-over planned short already does). A pull-over once begun keeps its ordinary pursuit. The car now stops 0.29 m over. | `test_emergency_late_pull_over.mjs` |

The first version of the second fix tightened the steering whenever a pull-over was not under way with
less than the full 8 m left. Over the suites it broke nine passing routes: Kitsilano city dry `s1-3` and
`s1-8`, fog `s1-8`, rain `s1-9` and `s1-11`, snow `s1-12`, and parking `s1-3` and `s1-8`. The audit fix
alone left all of them passing, so it was the steering. The plan is usually made a decision or two before
the car first chooses to pull over, so the shorter pursuit took over ordinary pull-overs. In rain `s1-9`
it also swung the car's nose into a parked car's 0.3 m margin, so the pull-over stayed rejected until
0.4 m was left; the ordinary pursuit, begun 6.2 m short, had got over. Limiting it to a pull-over not
begun a second after it was planned, with under half the steering distance left, restores all nine
and keeps the Vine Street fix.
