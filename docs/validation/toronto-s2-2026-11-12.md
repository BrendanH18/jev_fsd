## Bare suite

`scripts/bench_node.mjs` with default features, Kitsilano, suite seed 1: 12/12. The untouched tree
reproduces `20261111-130222-rules` field for field (timings aside). After the change `s1-1` and `s1-5`
are identical; the other ten routes change, all passing, from the turn-lane change alone (the bare
run is the same with and without the other three fixes, none of which runs in a bare world). Traffic
cars no longer plan turns they cannot reach the lane for, and every such choice changes where traffic
is for the rest of the drive. Summary: decisions 5,173 to 5,247, average speed 23.77 to 23.25 km/h,
safety brakes 7 to 6, minimum gap 1.4 to 1.0 m, minimum time to collision 0.7 to 1.2 s, RMS jerk 2.97
to 3.08. The largest change, `s1-2` (89.8 s to 142.8 s, stopped 1.8 s to 42.9 s), is timing: it waits
8 s at a stop sign on Balsam Street for crossing traffic, then catches two red lights on West Broadway
it used to make on green. The one new safety brake, `s1-4` at 61.9 s on Larch Street, is a slow meeting:
crawling through a junction at 0.7 m/s, the car sees the oncoming `car_p2`, 2.6 m to its left, as a car
1.1 m ahead; nothing touches. A new bare reference is needed once this lands.
