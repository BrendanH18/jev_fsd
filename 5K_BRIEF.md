## What counts as an improvement
- A real, user- or developer-visible change: a fix, a new option or report, stricter validation with a clear error, a robustness guard, a clearer message, an accessibility or UI fix, a doc correction tied to code, a small refactor that removes duplication, or a new regression check that pins down existing behaviour that had no test.
- At least 130 of your 200 must change shipped code (not only tests or docs). At most 70 may be regression coverage alone.
- Each must be distinct and checked. No filler, no renames for their own sake, no splitting one change into many entries.
